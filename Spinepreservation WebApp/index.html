60% of UK adults don't get enough magnesium from food alone—and serum blood tests miss it.
  • ✓ The 1% vs. 99% Rule: Only 1% of your body's magnesium circulates in your blood. The other 99% lives inside your bone (~60%) and skeletal muscle (~39%)—which is why replenishing deep tissue stores takes months, not days.
  • ✓ Daily Depleters: Stress, surgical recovery, caffeine, alcohol, and sweat drain intracellular magnesium every day. Stop topping up, and tissue levels slide back within weeks.

Why We Prescribe Magnesium Bisglycinate (300–400mg at Night)

Avoid cheap supermarket Magnesium Oxide (poorly absorbed). Binding elemental magnesium to the amino acid Glycine hits four spinal targets simultaneously:

  • Releases Paraspinal Muscle Spasm: Acts as a physiological calcium-channel blocker at the muscle spindle so tight multifidus and erector spinae fibres can unbind and relax.
  • Plugs the Dorsal Horn Pain Gate: Blocks spinal NMDA glutamate receptors to calm nerve "wind-up" and central sensitization.
  • Supplies Glycine for Disc Collagen & Sleep: Every 3rd amino acid in Type I disc collagen is Glycine—and glycine lowers core body temperature to trigger deep slow-wave sleep.
  • Activates Vitamin D3: Without sufficient magnesium, Vitamin D3 cannot be converted into its active hormone form to protect vertebral endplates.

The Mechanobiology of Spine Preservation

Why standard physiotherapy stretches fail—and how to keep your daily mechanical work inside your cellular capacity for repair.

01

The "Ankle Sprain" Clock

The outer ring of your disc (annulus fibrosus) is built from criss-crossing sheets of Type I collagen—just like your ankle ligaments. Even when acute spasm fades at Week 3, weak Type III scar tissue takes 3 to 6 months to remodel into strong Type I collagen.

Clinical Rule: Respect the 3–6 month protection window before resuming heavy axial impact or running.
02

Eliminate the #1 Enemy: Bending Forward

Bending forward (lumbar flexion) just 20–30 degrees turns your upper body into a heavy class-1 lever arm. Even without holding a weight, forward bending triples intradiscal pressure and wedges the fluid nucleus straight backward into the healing posterior tear.

Clinical Rule: Master the Hip Hinge. Bend at the hips and knees while keeping the lumbar spine neutral.
03

Feeding an Avascular Disc

Adult discs have no blood vessels. They breathe oxygen and flush out burning lactic acid through microscopic pores in the vertebral endplate. Static sitting clamps these pores shut, causing hypoxia and acidosis.

Clinical Rule: Combine 8 hrs flat sleep, a 15-min afternoon 90/90 Psoas Refill, and 1.5 Hz brisk walking pumps.
Scientific Exercise Prescription

Running vs. Cycling vs. Swimming vs. Isometrics

Moving beyond "do whatever doesn't hurt." Here is how each modality loads the intervertebral disc, facet joints, and spinal tendons.

Modality Biomechanical Effect on Disc & Facets Clinical Prescription
1. Brisk Walking Gentle sinusoidal pressure wave (~1.5–2 Hz) acts as a convective pump—squeezing lactic acid out and drawing oxygen and glucose across the endplates. Gold Standard from Day 1. 20–30 mins twice daily on flat ground with an upright chest.
2. Running Long-term runners develop thicker, more hydrated discs (higher glycosaminoglycans)—but high impact peaks will tear fragile Type III collagen during acute healing. Avoid for 3–6 months after an acute annular tear. Reintroduce gradually (+10%/week max) once collagen matures.
3. Cycling Drop-bar road bikes force sustained lumbar flexion—tilting the pelvis posteriorly and driving high pressure into posterior annular tears. (Unloads facet joints/stenosis). Disc Pain: Avoid road bikes; use an upright or recumbent bike with neutral lordosis.
Facet / Stenosis Pain: Excellent choice.
4. Swimming Buoyancy removes gravity, but Breaststroke hyperextends and jams facet joints, while turning the neck to breathe in Freestyle creates rotational annular shear. Avoid Breaststroke. Swim Backstroke, or do Front Crawl with a centre-mount swimmer's snorkel to eliminate twisting.
5. Isometric Core & Tendons "Bodybuilding" reps (sit-ups, twists) delaminate discs and irritate tendons. Core muscles are designed as isometric guy-wires; tendons heal best with heavy, slow isometric holds (1x/wk). McGill Big 3 (Side Plank, Bird-Dog, Modified Curl-up), Pallof presses, upright carries, and targeted isometric tendon holds.
Smart Pharmacology

The "5-Day Fire Extinguisher" Rule: Paracetamol vs. NSAIDs

Why we avoid chronic anti-inflammatories during the 3–6 month remodeling window—and when a short rescue pulse is clinically justified.

🟢 Paracetamol (Safe for Collagen Repair)

Acts centrally in the nervous system to take the edge off pain so you can sleep 8 hours and complete your daily walking pump—without blocking peripheral Prostaglandin E2 or collagen synthesis in healing discs and tendons.

🟡 Short-Course Ibuprofen / Etoricoxib (The 5-Day Rescue Pulse)

In an acute crisis (e.g., chemical sciatica from a disc leak or an acute facet joint flare stopping you from sleeping), a 3-to-7 day pulse of Etoricoxib or Ibuprofen acts as a fire extinguisher to break the acute spasm cycle.

🔴 Chronic Daily NSAIDs (Sabotages the Repair Envelope)

Taking NSAIDs week after week switches off COX-2 and Prostaglandin E2—the exact chemical triggers that instruct tenocytes, osteoblasts, and annular fibroblasts to build Type I collagen. Worse, it numbs your pain feedback loop so you overload an unhealed joint.

The 3-Tier Spine Preservation Stack

Supplying the structural bricks and cellular energy required for avascular disc cells and tendons to repair overnight.

Tier 1: Mandatory Structural Bricks

High Clinical Evidence
  • Vitamin C (500 mg Twice Daily — AM & PM): Essential co-factor for prolyl/lysyl hydroxylase to cross-link Type III into Type I collagen. Split dosing prevents plasma washout.
  • Magnesium Bisglycinate (300–400mg Evening): Releases paraspinal spasm, blocks spinal NMDA pain receptors, activates Vitamin D, and provides Glycine for collagen & deep sleep.
  • Vitamin D3 + K2 (MK-7): Directs calcium into bone while preventing calcification of the cartilaginous endplate "sieve."
  • Hydrolysed Collagen Peptides (10–15g): Taken 45 mins prior to isometric loading to double tendon/ligament synthesis.

Tier 2: Natural Pro-Resolving Support

Moderate Evidence
  • High-Dose Omega-3 (EPA/DHA 2–3g/day): Produces resolvins that clear residual inflammation without blunting collagen healing.

Tier 3: Cellular Longevity & Senolytics

Emerging Orthobiologic
  • NMN or NR (Morning NAD+ Precursor): Restores glycolytic energy in hypoxic disc cells and fuels SIRT1/SIRT3 repair enzymes.
  • Micronized Trans-Resveratrol: SIRT1 accelerator that inhibits cartilage-degrading MMP-13 and ADAMTS-4 enzymes.
  • Quercetin Phytosome: Natural senolytic that clears acid-secreting senescent ("zombie") disc cells.
