Section 02 • Pathophysiological Continuum

The "Bernard Theorem" & Our 433-Patient SPECT/CT Prevalence Data

In our analysis of 1,182 consecutive whole-body SPECT/CT scans (433 strictly asymptomatic oncology patients without bone metastases), focal osteoblastic uptake co-localizing with structural degeneration (normalized against baseline ASIS/iliac crest trabecular counts) was present in 89% of pain-free individuals[cite: 5]:

31–40 yrs56%
41–50 yrs68%
51–60 yrs89%
61–70 yrs95%
71–80 yrs92%

$\chi^2 = 48.076, p = 3.43 \times 10^{-9}$. Proves that "hot" subchondral turnover represents physiological remodeling within the repair envelope, not an automatic surgical pain generator.
